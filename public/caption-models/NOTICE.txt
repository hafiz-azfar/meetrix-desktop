Meetrix caption translation bundles the Apache-2.0 OPUS-MT English–Hindi and
English–Spanish models by Helsinki-NLP, converted to ONNX by Xenova.

Meetrix summary generation bundles the Apache-2.0 FLAN-T5 Small model
by Google, converted to ONNX by Xenova.

Original models
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-hi
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-es
https://huggingface.co/google/flan-t5-small
https://huggingface.co/Xenova/flan-t5-small

ONNX model revisions and integrity checks are recorded in scripts/stage-translation.mjs.
Transformers.js and Microsoft ONNX Runtime retain their upstream licenses.
